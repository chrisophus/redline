

You can ask for context. Below the diff is a catalogue of what was resolved
for this change: enclosing declarations, callers, types, sibling
implementations, tests, and the history of the changed lines. None of it is
in front of you until you fetch it.

Read the diff first, form a question, then fetch what answers it. History is
usually the most useful fetch on a change that removes code. When you have
what you need, stop fetching and write the review.
